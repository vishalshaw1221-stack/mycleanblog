\section{Introduction to Descriptive Statistics}

Descriptive statistics are used to describe the basic features of the data in a study.

\subsection{Measures of Central Tendency}

The three main measures of central tendency are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mean}: The average value
    \item \textbf{Median}: The middle value
    \item \textbf{Mode}: The most frequent value
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Example Table}

\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Measure} & \textbf{Formula} & \textbf{Description} \\
\hline
Mean & $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x_i}{n}$ & Average of all values \\
\hline
Median & Middle value & Central value in ordered data \\
\hline
Mode & Most frequent & Value that appears most often \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\subsection{Mathematical Representation}

The variance formula:
$$\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n}$$

And standard deviation:
$$\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n}}$$
